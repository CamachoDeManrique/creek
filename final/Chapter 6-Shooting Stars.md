**Chapter 6**

**Tweek- Shooting Stars** 

The sun came through the window in a long stripe across the bed. He’d been lying in the same spot for so long the sunlight was starting to burn his back through his shirt. Tweek turned onto his side and moved a little closer to the edge of the bed, his fingers tightening around the comic book Craig had handed him. He’d been reading it since they got home from school. On the floor beside the bed, Craig shifted as he carefully built a new robot model. 

“You guys are filthy liars.” Tweek looked up. Kenny stood in the doorway with his little sister Karen next to him. He put his hand on her head and tilted his chin toward the hallway. “Go play with Tricia really quick. I gotta talk to these guys.”

“I thought you said we were all playing together,” Karen said, crossing her little arms and looking up at him with a frown. 

“Sure. But first I gotta talk to them. Give me five minutes.”

“But I don't even know her.” Karen pouted, stomping her foot. 

“Just go talk about girl stuff.” Kenny placed his hand on her shoulder and gently pushed her.

“When did we start doing playdates?” Craig asked without looking up, adjusting a plastic arm on the model. “And who let you in?”

“Shut up.” Kenny mumbled, glancing down the hall before closing the door securely behind him. He turned to face them, crossing his arms. “Ok, I gotta know something. Cuz you guys up on stage singing and holding hands makes it look like you're both actually gay. And there is no way you two were ever dating in the first place.” He pointed to Craig. “You only care about your guinea pig and Red Racer, and you—” He pointed at Tweek. “You’ve got way too much anxiety to keep a secret.”

Craig shrugged and kept working.

“I can keep a secret.” Tweek sat up, closing the comic book. “I just don't like to.”

“Uh-huh. Sure.” Kenny rolled his eyes. “I was honestly waiting for you guys to break up again, but it looks like it’s not happening. So tell me the truth.”

Craig stood up and placed the model carefully on his desk.

“We really don't care if you do or don't believe us." He turned to look at Kenny. “Everyone else just has to believe it.”

“Ok, but I care. I really need to know.”

“Why do you think we’re not gay?” 

Kenny didn’t answer right away. Instead, he pulled his parka off completely and let it hit the floor. A mess of straight blonde hair, cut in uneven, awkward lengths, popped out. He spread his arms open wide, a stained white shirt with small holes clinging to his skinny body. 

“Behold and rejoice\!” Kenny declared, strutting around the room.

“Please don’t get naked in my room,” Craig said, crossing his arms flat over his chest. 

Kenny dropped his hand to his sides and quickly closed the distance, leaning directly into Craig’s face.

“Aha\! See? Not gay\! Any gay guy would never ask another guy *not* to get naked.”

Craig scoffed, and Tweek let out a small laugh under his breath.

“That proves nothing.” Tweek said, letting his legs dangle over the edge of the bed. “Just proves we don’t want to see you naked.”

“Ok,” Kenny placed his hands on his hips. “Then both of you kiss.”

“No way, dude.” Craig shook his head, turning away. “We’re not perverts like you.”

“I’m not gay for *you*, sorry.”

“Kenny, are you gay?” Tweek asked, tilting his head. “Since… it sounds like you know a lot about gay people.”

“Fuck no, I love women.” Kenny stood up and walked to grab his parka. “Long story short— I like one of the Asian girls so I pretended to like gay stuff so she would talk to me.”

“Sounds about right,” Craig muttered, leaning back in his chair. Kenny suddenly reached down and snatched Craig’s hat. “Hey\!” 

“Oh, but she’s not the one that drew you guys together.” Kenny playfully slapped Craig’s arm with the chullo hat. “That was the other Asian girls. She was more into drawing Stan and Kyle together.”

Both Craig and Tweek nod at the same time, as if that made perfect sense.

“Ok, fine. We’re not gay. Not that it matters.” Craig twirled the chair to face the desk and flipped open his laptop. “You can do whatever you want with the information.”

“I’ll keep your dirty secret.” Kenny tossed the hat on Craig’s desk and put his parka back on. “I really just wanted to prove I was right.” He tugged the hood up and looked at Tweek. “When Wendy was showing all that art of you guys weeks ago, I already knew there was no way in hell you guys were actually dating.” he zipped up his parka all the way over his mouth. “But you guys haven’t broken up again after the first time. Then I saw you guys at the theme park…and then you both on stage today. Yeah, it looked pretty gay.”

A sudden prickly heat spiked behind Tweek’s ears, spreading across his cheeks before he could stop it. Craig glanced over his shoulder. 

“You done?” he asked Kenny before swiping his hat back and pulling it over. 

“I just wanted to let you guys know, you don't gotta pretend to be gay around me. I’m sure you guys have your reasons to be butt buddies.”

“Please don't ever call us that.” Craig said as he glared at Kenny who started to giggle.

“Well, thanks Kenny.” Tweek said, a smile pulling at his lips. “That’s actually really nice of you.”

“Yeah, no problem.” Kenny plopped down beside Tweek on the bed. “And if you guys ever want to actually practice sucking di—”

“No\!” both Craig and Tweek yelled at the same time.

“I’m just saying\! I’m here for you guys. Geez.”

Craig reached for the nearest thing and threw it at Kenny’s head. Tweek laughed when it actually hit Kenny. 

A low vibration moved through the room, quiet at first. He turned to look at Craig and Kenny but it didn’t seem like they heard it. Then the bed under him trembled, the walls vibrated, the sound was pressed hard against his eardrums. Tweek placed his hands over his ears. He closed his eyes. The vibrating sound was getting louder. 

When Tweek opened his eyes, he was in the school library. 

*What... What happened?*

Tweek blinked, waiting for the blurred edges of the room to snap into focus. He shifted his weight on the chair, his bones feeling stiff and his eyes burning. His chest ached, compressed by an unseen pressure. He leaned back in the hard chair and pressed a hand to his forehead. 

*Was that a dream? No… not a dream. I remember that happening.* 

His stomach dropped. He leaned over. His concentration then shattered by the vibration on the table. It was coming from his phone. He quickly picked it up and realized it was an alarm. He rubbed his eyes as he turned it off. 

*I don’t remember setting an alarm…did I?* 

Pressure started building behind his eyes, settling into a throbbing pulse behind his eyebrows. 

*I lost time. I was just at lunch… Kenny was next to me. How did I fall asleep? I never fall asleep when I lose time. No, no… breathe… it’s ok.* 

Tweek closed his eyes and placed a hand over his chest. He started to take deep breaths. He forced his eyes open.

*Grounding. I can’t lose time again. I need to do some grounding.* 

He grabbed his phone and slowly stood up, his hand gripping the edge of the table. He didn’t let go until his knees locked into place. 

*Three things I can see.* 

He looked at the old clock on the wall—the one that was always broken. Then to the book cart that was shoved in the corner of the room, overflowing with books that needed to be shelved. Finally, his eyes landed on the old librarian at her desk. Her white hair falling neatly over her shoulders. She looked at him as he walked up.

“Oh, good\! You woke up,” she said, her voice a low murmur. “Craig told me to wake you up in case the alarm didn’t work.”

“Craig was with m-me?” His shoulders dropped slightly, his fingers instantly gripping the hem of his shirt.

“Well, of course he was.” She looked back down at her book in her hand. “You two are practically joined at the hip. Can’t see one without the other.”

*Craig set my alarm. Of course he did.* 

The sudden pressure in his chest replaced the tightness in his stomach.

“Do you… do you know where he went?” Tweek’s shoulder twitched sharply. “Did he say?”

“No, sorry sugar. He wasn’t here for long, though.”

“Ok, thank you Mrs…um… Mrs…” 

Tweek’s voice broke apart into a frustrated stutter as he twisted the fabric of his shirt. The librarian waited patiently, a sad smile moving over her face. Her eyes flicked down to Tweek’s mouth—to his scar. He pressed his lips together tightly, trying to hide it. Her expression softened into pity. 

“Mrs. Miller,” she said gently, closing her book and leaning forward. “Now go on and get to your next class. Free period is over.”

Tweek nodded and walked out of the library. His hands started to tremble as he stepped into the busy hallway. An uncontrollable itch crawled through the muscles of his legs. He could feel people turning to look at him.

*Oh God, do I have something on my face? Did I drool?*

Tweek raised his hands to his mouth and hair, checking for anything out of place. Suddenly, Clyde popped up beside him.

“Yo, sleeping beauty. Headed to your locker?”

Tweek flinched at his sudden appearance.

“Oh, Clyde... Y-yeah. I guess so.”

Tweek stopped at the base of the stairs, swallowing hard and gripping the center of his shirt as he began to climb. People brushed past him, their shoulders bumping his with an unnecessary force. When he reached the landing, it felt like every pair of eyes in the hallway was tracking him, watching him with a wary curiosity. Someone slammed hard against his shoulder, nearly knocking him off balance, but before Tweek could react, Stan moved in, shoving the person back.

“The fuck? You can’t walk without bumping into people?” Stan snapped, stepping into line beside Tweek. 

*Ah Jesus\! Just breathe… just breathe.* 

When Tweek reached his locker, the weight of the collective gazes felt like it was burning a hole in the back of his head. Once he grabbed his backpack and turned around he noticed Kyle and Cartman were standing behind him, throwing glares at anyone who lingered too long in their direction.

“Oh, hey.” Tweek said, his voice small as his shoulder twitched. “What— what are you guys doing here?”

“Oh… just wanted to walk with you.” Kyle said, stepping beside him. “Ready?”

Tweek’s eyebrows lifted for a brief second before he nodded. Stan and Clyde fell in directly behind him. Cartman moved ahead, cutting a path through the crowd, while Kyle stayed at his side. As they walked down the hall Tweek noticed how they never broke their formation.

*Why are they all here? Are people mad at me?* 

The panic was rising like hot bubbling water inside his chest. Someone laughed down the hall. His stomach twisted in a painful knot. Was that about him? Did he do something embarrassing during the time he lost? Did he hit someone?

Last year, on the first day of school, he lost time too. 

But Craig had been there. 

He didn’t need to worry about not remembering when Craig was there.

Tweek turned his head slightly, glancing at Kyle.

“Have you seen Craig?” 

Kyle shook his head, looking straight ahead.

“No, I haven't seen him since lunch.”

*Lunch?* 

He couldn’t remember seeing Craig at lunch. He scanned the crowded hallway and didn’t see him. Something happened at lunch. He was trying to go back into his memory. Flipping pages and finding only blank spaces. 

He remembers Candie. 

She sat down next to him. 

Something happened

Then a total blackout. 

He shook his head to clear the static. No, it was gone. Nothing was coming back. Not even a glimpse of Craig in the lunchroom. 

*Why am I looking for Craig? I told him to stay away. I don’t need him. I can be ok without him.* 

He closed his eyes for a second, trying to steady the world as it tilted around him.

He opened them.

A classroom.

Tweek jumped a little in his seat, his breath coming in quick, shallow gasps, his hands trembling. Right on cue.

The hallway was gone.

He was surrounded by the hum of the teacher’s lecture.

*Oh God… Oh God… what happened? Why? Why did I lose time again?*

Tweek closed his eyes and forced a deep, ragged breath into his lungs, trying desperately to calm down. He let out a shaking breath while trying to hold the rest of it together. Slowly, the voices that had blended into the white noise began to separate.

“So, he’s never been without Craig?” Marla asked. Her whisper was right next to him. 

“Well, yeah, I mean, they got their own lives and shit, but at school? Nah, Craig was with him all the time,” Clyde answered her. “Dude was basically his shadow.”

*Are they talking about me?* 

“Shit dude, I just realized.” Stan’s voice cut in, sounding a little farther away. “This year will be the first time Tweek’s alone at school since the accident. The whole school’s a trigger.”

“Tweek’s accident happened at school?” Marla asked.

Silence.

The teacher’s voice, present somewhere in front, continued over them. 

Tweek lowered his head onto his desk. It suddenly felt too heavy to hold up. A wave of nausea washed over him.

*I think I need to puke.*

He raised a shaking hand and forced his gaze upward. He didn’t recognize the teacher who turned to look at him.

“Yes, Mr. Tweak?” she asked, turning toward his row in surprise.

“Can I please go—” His voice sounded scratchy and uneven. “Can I go to the restroom?”

The teacher only nodded and turned back to the whiteboard. Tweek gripped at the desk’s edge and slowly stood up. A sharp, localized pain flared at the bottom of his stomach, his hand quickly pressed flat against his abdomen.

“Dude, you need help?” Clyde asked quietly.

Tweek shook his head automatically and walked slowly out of the classroom. He could feel everyone’s eyes trailing behind him.

The moment he stepped into the quiet hallway, he felt unsteady. He placed his hand on the wall to stop the hallways from rocking back and forth. He tried to walk quickly to the restroom. A hot, burning sensation suddenly clawed its way up his chest and into his throat. He burst through the heavy door of the empty restroom and ran into the nearest stall. He fell on his knees, gripping the toilet seat as his body violently heaved forward. 

Nothing came out.

He shook his head, forcing his eyes shut as he started to take deep breaths. 

*No. Please. I don't want to puke. It hurts…*

After a few more deep breaths the pressure in his throat faded a little. He looked up at the ceiling and closed his eyes. Slowly, his stomach felt like it was settling back into its proper place in his body. He opened his eyes and slowly stood up, using the back of his hand to wipe the sweat from his mouth. 

He was leaning against the stall wall, waiting for the dizziness to clear a little more, when he heard two boys walk in. He froze, holding his breath.

“I’m telling you, I bet ten bucks on Candie having a crush on Tweek.” one boy said, his voice unfamiliar. “Why else would Craig pull that shit at lunch?”

“No way, Tweek’s too fucked up. I bet twenty bucks Candie isn’t into him,” the second boy’s voice sounded younger. “Craig’s the only one who wants Tweek since it's his fault.”

“No it wasn't. It was Trevor and his brothers.”

*Trevor?*

Tweek’s head tilted to the side, a heaviness pulling it down toward his shoulder. The name felt wrong. Like a curse word that could get you in trouble. There was a wave of violent tremors that rippled through Tweek’s body. It felt like ice water poured through him from the inside, settling cold and heavy in his stomach. 

A high-pitched ringing pierced his ears—so loud he clasped his hands over them. He heard the faint sound of flushing and running water. The voices muffled, distant. 

The ringing didn’t stop until the restroom was quiet and he was alone again. Tweek walked shakily out of the stall and stepped up to the sink. He turned the faucet on, washing his trembling hands before splashing water over his face. His reflection looked pale under the fluorescent light. The scar on his bottom lip, bright pink against his skin. He shook his head.

*Who’s Trevor?* 

As Tweek walked back to class he sorted through his memories.

*Was he a new kid? But they said he had brothers. When did a bunch of kids show up to South Park? Craig would know. I need to remember to ask him. Yeah, remember to ask him.* 

He stood outside the classroom door, his hand reaching for the metal handle before pausing.

*Wait… would Craig even tell me? Did they say Trevor did something to me?* 

He blinked.

The room shifted again. 

His eyes burned and felt dry. He blinked over and over, trying to force the tears flooding his eyes to help. His jaw was locked tight, the muscles sore. His joints felt wrong. A crawling, biting sensation just under the skin that he couldn’t reach. He let out a quivering breath that vibrated his throat as it came out. His lungs began to heave as the walls of the classroom seemed to lean inward. 

“That’s not a rabbit hole, he’s spacing out,” Kyle said, leaning back from Tweek’s desk.

The sudden conversation pulled him back.

*How does Kyle know about—* 

“I’ve seen him space out,” Marla added, leaning across Tweek to look at Kyle from the other side. “This is something different.”

“The text says there’s only three things to look for.” Stan said, “Either Tweek is spacing out, going down a rabbit hole or losing time.”

“I don't know,” Stan said. “But doesn’t he need a trigger or something to start either of them?”
